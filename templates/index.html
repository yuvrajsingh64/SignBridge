How It Works

1
Capture

Upload an image, use your webcam, or upload a short video showing an ASL hand sign.

2
Process

The image is resized, normalized, and fed into a Convolutional Neural Network trained on ASL alphabet images.

3
Classify

The CNN outputs probabilities for each ASL class. The highest-confidence prediction is selected.

4
Translate

The predicted sign is mapped to its English meaning and displayed along with the confidence score.

Supported Signs

The model recognizes 29 ASL classes: 26 letters + 3 special gestures.

Official ASL Alphabet Reference Chart (A - Z)

For best accuracy, hold your hand in front of the camera matching the exact posture shown in this standard chart.

Letters J and Z are dynamic signs (involve motion). The model uses static images only, so recognition of these letters may be less reliable.

A college-level AI/ML project. Built with TensorFlow, OpenCV, Flask, and Bootstrap.

This tool recognizes individual ASL alphabet signs from static images. It does not translate complete ASL sentences or grammar.
